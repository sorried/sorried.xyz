# изображения и шрифты

`sky.webp` и `window.webp` созданы для этой страницы встроенным инструментом imagegen. Ни изображения, ни маски из Posy не использовались. PNG переведены в WebP с качеством 85 без изменения размера. `avatar.svg`, `paper.svg` и значки в `index.html` нарисованы кодом для этой реализации.

Запрос для `sky.webp`:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original full-screen painted sky backdrop for a personal link-in-bio webpage. Create a landscape 3:2 watercolor and opaque gouache illustration on textured cold-press paper. A deep clear muted cerulean blue sky fills the upper two thirds, with large billowing warm ivory cumulus clouds edged with dusty lavender gathering across the middle and lower right. Along the very bottom, a distant rolling green countryside, tiny warm terracotta rooftops and a few telephone wires. A tiny cream-colored diamond kite with a short rust red ribbon in the upper right. Soft sunlit late afternoon, nostalgic hand-painted picture-book atmosphere, visible paint brush textures, rich blue and subtle grain. Keep the center mostly open sky and clouds as interface panels will sit above the illustration. Make a fully new original composition. No text, no letters, no UI, no logos, no watermark. Save the generated output as an image asset.

Запрос для `window.webp`:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original landscape illustration for a framed picture on a personal webpage, landscape 3:2. A small black cat with warm golden eyes curled up on a sunlit old wooden window sill with a small potted plant, viewed from inside a quiet little countryside attic. Beyond the open window is a wide cerulean blue sky, a big fluffy cream cloud, green hills, and a few tiny terracotta rooftops. A linen curtain hangs at the left edge. Traditional watercolor and gouache on cold-press paper, delicate pencil linework, visible brush marks and grain, muted teal greens, warm honey and soft ivory, intimate storybook mood. Carefully drawn natural cat anatomy. No people, no letters, no text, no logos, no border, no watermark. Make a fully new original composition, not based on an existing artwork.

Шрифты [Caveat](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/caveat) и [Cormorant Garamond](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond) загружены из Google Fonts. Латинский и кириллический наборы хранятся в `fonts/`, лицензии SIL Open Font License сохранены рядом. Страница не обращается к Google Fonts при открытии.
